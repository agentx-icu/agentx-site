A SMALL PROMISE一个小小的承诺

Make it useful.
Make it yours.做有用的事。
把它交给你。

01

Open by default默认开放

Source, protocols, and release notes stay in the open so you can understand what runs on your devices.源代码、协议和发布说明都公开，让你了解设备上运行的一切。

02

Private by design隐私优先

We favour peer-to-peer paths and local-first experiences over collecting more data.优先选择点对点和本地优先的体验，而不是收集更多数据。

03

Small, then better先小，再变好

Focused tools can evolve with their communities. Every project is a work in progress.专注的工具可以和社区一起成长。每个项目都在持续改进。
